## Full description

LesiSearch helps you find used vehicles in Sri Lanka by searching popular listing sites and ranking matches with AI.

**Features**
- Search without signing in
- Filters for make, model, location, year, and price
- AI-ranked top picks with listing details
- Open listings in an in-app browser
- Optional email alerts (guest trial or signed-in)
- Profile and alert filter management

**Notes**
- Rankings are informational and may be incomplete or outdated.
- Listing content belongs to third-party sites such as ikman.lk and riyasewana.com.
- Always verify details with the seller before you buy.

Website: https://www.lesisearch.com
